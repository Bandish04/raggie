# Trademark policy

The Raggie source code is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).
That license covers the code. It does **not** grant rights to the name "Raggie"
or the Raggie logo (Apache-2.0, section 6). This policy explains how you may use
them.

The goal is simple: anyone should be able to tell whether something comes from
the Raggie project. Use the name to talk about Raggie freely; do not use it in a
way that makes people think your product, service or fork *is* Raggie or is
endorsed by the project.

## Uses that are always fine

You do not need permission to:

- **Refer to Raggie** in text, talks, articles, documentation, comparisons and
  reviews ("we evaluated Raggie", "a Helm chart for Raggie", "compatible with
  Raggie 0.x").
- **Say that your work is built on Raggie** or contains it: "powered by Raggie",
  "based on Raggie", "a fork of Raggie". Use the name as plain text and make
  clear that it is your work.
- **Run Raggie** in your organization, under your own brand, for your own users.
  Raggie has white-label branding built in (organization name, logo, colors);
  replacing the Raggie name and logo in the UI is welcome.
- **Distribute unmodified Raggie** (source or the published images) under the
  Raggie name, as long as you keep the license and notices.
- **Use the unmodified logo** to link to the project or to illustrate an article,
  talk or listing about Raggie.

This is nominative fair use: using the name only as much as you need to identify
the project, without implying more.

## Uses that are not allowed

- **Implying endorsement or affiliation.** Do not suggest that the Raggie project
  or its maintainers sponsor, certify, support or partner with your product,
  service or company unless they have agreed to it in writing.
- **Distributing a modified fork as a product under the Raggie name.** If you
  materially modify Raggie and distribute it, or offer it as a product or hosted
  service for others, give it a different name. You may say it is "based on
  Raggie". Material changes include altering features or behavior, removing
  security controls, or rebranding parts of it. Packaging changes (a different
  base image, build flags, a config preset) and backports of upstream fixes are
  not material.
- **Confusingly similar names.** Do not use a name, logo, domain name or social
  media handle that is likely to be confused with Raggie, such as "Raggie Cloud",
  "Raggie Enterprise" or "RaggieAI" for something that is not the project's own.
- **Modifying the logo.** Use the logo only as published: no recoloring,
  distortion, cropping, animation, or combining it with other marks. Do not use
  it as your product's or company's logo or icon.
- **Misleading use.** Do not use the name or logo in a way that is false,
  deceptive or disparaging, or for content that violates the
  [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Merchandise and events

Non-commercial items (stickers, t-shirts for a meetup) that use the unmodified
logo are fine. Selling merchandise, or naming an event or conference after
Raggie, needs written permission.

## Asking for permission

If you are unsure whether a use is allowed, or you want to do something this
policy does not allow, open an issue or contact the project lead
(see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)). The project may update this policy; the
version in the main branch applies.
