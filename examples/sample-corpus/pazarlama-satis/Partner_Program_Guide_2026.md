## 1. Purpose

The Nebula Partner Program helps consultancies, system integrators and regional resellers sell, implement and support NebulaCRM. This guide sets out the partner tiers, the requirements to reach and keep each tier, the commercial terms, and the rules for deal registration. It replaces the 2025 partner handbook. Questions go to partners@nebulacrm.example; all registrations and certifications are handled in the partner portal at partner.nebulacrm.example.

## 2. Partner tiers

There are four tiers: Registered, Silver, Gold and Platinum. Every new partner starts at Registered after signing the partner agreement and completing onboarding. Tiers are reviewed once a year in January based on the previous calendar year's results; a partner that exceeds the next tier's thresholds mid-year may request an early upgrade, which takes effect on the first day of the following month.

| Tier | Certified consultants | Annual partner-sourced bookings | Customer CSAT | Resale margin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Registered | 1 | none | none | 10% |
| Silver | 3 | TRY 1,500,000 | ≥ 85% | 15% |
| Gold | 10 | TRY 4,000,000 | ≥ 88% | 20% |
| Platinum | 20 | TRY 10,000,000 | ≥ 90% | 25% |

Table 1. Tier requirements and resale margins. Margins apply to the list price of NebulaCRM subscriptions and add-on modules.

Bookings count the first-year contract value of new customers and expansions that the partner sourced and registered. Renewals count at 50% of their value. CSAT is measured through the post-implementation survey sent to the customer's project owner.

Market development funds (MDF) are accrued quarterly and must be used within the following two quarters for jointly approved activities such as events, webinars and localized content. Unused MDF does not roll over.

## 3. Deal registration

1. The partner registers the opportunity in the partner portal before the first customer demo, with the customer's legal name, expected user count and plan.
2. Nebula approves or rejects the registration within 2 business days.
3. An approved registration protects the opportunity for 90 days. It can be extended once by 30 days if the partner shows documented progress.
4. If two partners register the same opportunity, the first complete registration wins.
5. Registered deals that close within the protection period earn an extra 5 percentage points of margin on the first-year contract value.

Direct sales will not pursue an opportunity that a partner has registered and that is under protection. Opportunities that the customer brings directly to Nebula before any registration remain with direct sales.

## 4. Commercial terms

- Resale: the partner buys at list price minus its tier margin and invoices the customer.
- Referral only: partners that do not resell receive a referral fee of 10% of the first-year contract value, paid after the customer's first payment.
- Additional customer discounts on top of the partner margin follow Nebula's direct sales discount authority matrix in the current price list.
- Nebula invoices partners monthly; payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date.
- Partners in Germany, Austria and Switzerland contract with Nebula Technology GmbH and buy from the EUR price list.

## 5. Certification

Partner consultants become Nebula Certified Consultants by passing the online certification exam. The passing score is 75% and the certificate is valid for 24 months. The exam fee is TRY 4,500 per attempt and is waived for Gold and Platinum partners. A consultant counts toward only one partner's tier at a time.

Implementation projects for Kurumsal and Kurumsal Plus customers must be led by a certified consultant. Nebula's Customer Success team may review any project and can require a remediation plan if the customer's CSAT falls below the tier threshold.

## 6. Conduct and termination

Partners must follow Nebula's ethics rules, including the gift and hospitality limits, when dealing with customers and Nebula employees. Either party may terminate the partner agreement with 60 days' written notice. On termination, customers keep their subscriptions and are transferred to direct sales or to another partner of their choice.
